Welcome To Agrasain Balika Siksha Sadan

AIMS AND OBJECTIVES:
The institution aims at complete personality development of all its students.
Earnest efforts are made:
 To promote all round development – physical, intellectual, cultural, social and spiritual of all students.
 To foster in them a sense of discipline, decorum and devotion to superiors.
 To promote appreciation of the dignity of labor and the importance of national integration.
 To train them to live in harmony and fellowship with their neighbors’ co workers and others.
 To inculcate in them the spirit of service to society, specially the under-privileged sections.
 To build up their total personality so that they may become honest, responsible and patriotic citizens of Mahan Bharat.
